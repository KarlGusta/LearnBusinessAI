# PRODUCT.md

## What this is
Personal learning dashboard for one user: an agency founder serving small
and medium businesses, building the capability to deliver the customer-facing
AI that businesses will adopt the way they adopted websites, phone numbers,
email and social accounts. Capability first; the sellable service line is
expected to emerge from the shipped stations along the way.

## Source
Derived from the Zuckerberg interview on the future of AI (Cleo Abram, Huge
Conversations, 2024): every business will have an AI that sells and supports;
many AIs, not one; open weights as the ownership path; personalized means it
has context; keep up or be outcompeted. Phase goals quote these theses.

## Structure
102 stations, 60 minutes each, 9 phases in two terms. Short term (phases
1-5): from transformer fundamentals to a hardened, evaluated conduit live on
a real site across web, WhatsApp, Instagram, email and an MCP endpoint.
Long term (phases 6-9): fine-tuning and owned weights, GEO discovery,
ambient and voice readiness on simulated hardware, then productization and
the first paying client.

## Constraints honored
- No discrete GPU: free GPU rotation for training, CPU inference for serving.
- No hardware budget: ambient phase is simulated (Home Assistant in Docker,
  Wokwi, WebXR emulator, Alexa simulator).
- Free first: nearly all stations free; the two WhatsApp production
  stations carry the paid flag honestly.
- Hybrid assets: phases end in keepable artifacts (eval sets, MCP servers,
  fine-tunes, runbooks) only where the logic says so; no force-fitting.

## Non-goals
Not a course marketplace, not a general AI curriculum. Every station either
serves the conduit thesis or the operator's readiness for whatever device
comes next.
